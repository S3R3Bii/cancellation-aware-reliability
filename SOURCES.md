# Sources and prior-work limits

- BTS official monthly export: https://www.transtats.bts.gov/ot_delay/OT_DelayCause1.asp . Eight requested fields confirmed in the official export; exact count controls must pass before analysis; a protocol is not an analyzed result.
- BTS reporting definitions: https://www.bts.gov/topics/airlines-and-airports/understanding-reporting-causes-flight-delays-and-cancellations . Operating-carrier and marketing-network reporting differ; reported causes are broad categories, not verified causal attribution.
- Bratu and Barnhart, An Analysis of Passenger Delays Using Flight Operations and Passenger Booking Data, https://doi.org/10.2514/atcq.13.1.1 . Flight-based punctuality does not capture passenger delays from cancellations/missed connections. This study has no bookings or passenger-delay claims.
- Seelhorst, Flight Cancellation Behavior and Aviation System Performance (2014), https://escholarship.org/uc/item/3045w49k . Cancellation selection and its relation to flight-delay forecasts are established research topics; this study does not reproduce a queueing/cancellation-choice model or establish a literature gap.
- DOT source rights policy/metadata must be verified before publication; factual counts and original outputs only, without full source reports or third-party expressive material. No legal opinion or universal rights claim.

- Bounded factual-data publication scope and carrier reporting qualification: [pre-projection clarification](SOURCE_CLARIFICATION.md). No full source reports/archives or blanket source license.
