# Cancellation-aware airport reliability: fixed retrospective design

## Decision and acceptance

How much can an on-time percentage change when cancelled and diverted flights are removed from its denominator? The deliverable is a complete finite comparison of conditional and scheduled-flight metrics, fixed airport-mix standardization and explicit synthetic delayed-to-cancelled conversions. It describes captured published flight-count aggregates; it does not measure passenger welfare, rebooking, actual cancellation strategy or a causal operational benefit.

Success requires the protocol before analytical count retrieval; exact official export, schema/rights/count definitions and source reconciliation; independent parsing, complete cohort/exclusion records, all finite comparisons and frozen executable design before held summaries; independent SQL and exact arithmetic plus isolated replay; executive-readable findings with actual limitations, original checked figures and rendered independent manuscript; scoped publication/canonical Git checks, exact-head Linux CI, matching merged tree and annotated remote tag. A required unknown or failure blocks release. Never call a source hold a finished study.

## Source and scoped design

Use the official BTS monthly airline-airport delay-cause export for January 2019 through December 2025. Only source landing/form metadata and general national August 2026 display values have been seen at this point; no study-window analytical counts have been retrieved. Inspect the first authorized ZIP header after this commit, document any exact aliases before projecting values and verify scheduled-total and outcome definitions against the official tabular display. Require unique year/month/carrier/airport keys and nonnegative integer counts with delayed+cancelled+diverted no greater than total. Null/absent/zero are distinct. Do not infer a source-free complete schedule or actual passenger delay. Capture raw archive/member hashes and selected projection controls. Raw ZIPs and full third-party source text stay private. Publish only a minimal factual aggregate projection after source rights pass, original work and a bounded rights statement; no unrestricted license for third-party material is implied.

The fixed carriers are AA, DL, UA and WN, as operational codes in the source. Marketing networks and code-share totals are not mixed with them or added to a national total. Require exact code identity and disclose reporting-scope changes. Select all airports with twelve valid positive scheduled-total entries per carrier in baseline 2019 for all four carriers; require at least five. No outcome-rate screen is allowed. For each carrier-year retain only baseline airports with twelve valid positive-total months in that year, preserving every excluded airport/reason and its baseline scheduled-volume share. A missing entry is not zero or verified service exit.

Baseline weights are each airport's share of total 2019 scheduled flights across the four carriers in the baseline cohort. For each carrier-year panel renormalize those fixed weights on that panel. Compute both observed-count pooled rates and directly standardized rates; standardization controls this recorded airport mix only. It does not equalize origin-destination routes, seasonal schedules, passengers, weather or other confounders. Changing panels change the comparison population; show coverage and never disguise it as a fixed national benchmark.

Let N be verified reported scheduled total, D the noncancelled/nondiverted arrival-delay count (at least fifteen minutes), C cancelled and V diverted. O=N-D-C-V is the residual on-time category; B=N-C-V is the completed-at-destination denominator. Compare D/B, (D+C+V)/N, O/B, O/N, C/N, V/N and O/B-O/N. Both metrics answer different questions. Preserve the exact denominator and units (fractions/percentage points). This is a competing categorical-outcome description, not a fitted survival model, a threshold forecast or inferred time-to-event analysis.

Development carriers are AA and DL, development target years 2020–2022. Primary held panels are UA and WN in 2023–2025; retain all other fixed carrier-year results as declared secondary descriptions. Baseline 2019 weights include each carrier's baseline counts and therefore do not constitute unseen-carrier prediction. Source inputs/quality are visible without blinding; freeze source/protocol/model/checker before held analytical summaries. No fitting, optimizer, model winner or post-held tuning. All parameters are untuned design choices and not validated operating rules.

For each valid cell apply all q={0,1/10,1/4,1/2}: convert floor(q*D) delayed flights to cancellations. Hold N,V,O constant; reduce D and increase C by that integer. Retain every scenario. Check identities: scheduled disruption and scheduled on-time are unchanged; completed-flight on-time cannot decrease; zero q is exact identity. If B becomes zero, conditional metrics are explicitly undefined and excluded only from those conditional summaries with full reasons. This is a mechanical denominator stress; it does not establish that airlines cancelled particular delayed flights or how passengers were affected.

The executive first layer must state the decision, actual finite measured finding, required next evidence and limitations. No peer-review, general novelty, causal or future coverage claim. Prior work already studies cancellation selection and passenger-delay limits, so the contribution is a transparent finite reproducible comparison, not a new idea.

The original proposal's route standardization, weather adjustment and survival-analysis skill claims are not supported by this monthly airport export and are outside this scoped release. Those extensions require a separate prospective protocol and sufficient data. Reviewed independent program manuscripts may be public; Azure optimization and other formal-publication projects remain private.
